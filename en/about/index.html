About

Hi, I'm Junyong Moon

Who am I

Ever since I was young, I have liked planning things. At university I went through three majors before choosing computer science, where I could build what I imagined. It was a long way around, and it taught me not to rush.

I care about building services people enjoy using, and I value a sense for what truly matters.

Now and then I set off somewhere unfamiliar and play music I love. That is how I find my energy again.

Introduce

I started this blog to keep what I wanted to remember from my studies. Now I also write down the thoughts I have along the way.

Working as a developer, I found there is no end to what to learn, and the ground keeps shifting. So rather than comparing myself with others, I try to keep my own pace, focus on the present and move forward little by little.

I also think attitude matters as much as skill. Developers sometimes work alone, but mostly we build services as a team with colleagues from many roles. So besides notes on technology, I record what I learn from working together and how I want to work as a developer.

This blog holds one developer's growth and thoughts. It is a plain and honest record rather than polished writing, so I hope you read it lightly, thinking "so there are developers like this, too."

Someday, when I read these records again, I hope the road behind me looks beautiful.

Technical Writing

The diagram is in Korean; the lists below say the same.

Building my own writing pipeline

  • Pick a topic, keep it in Notion and study around it in depth.
  • When drafting, set a time limit and turn on do-not-disturb.
  • After publishing, keep revising and updating.

Writing that is easy to understand and to read

  • Keep each post to a reasonable length.
  • Use pictures and other visuals for complex ideas.
  • Write down why a choice was made, not only what was done.
